# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in RustO2 to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Accepting feedback on code and ideas gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy towards other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions) and to public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer by opening a private security
advisory at <https://github.com/togioia/RustO2/security/advisories/new> with the
subject prefixed by `[Code of Conduct]`. All reports are reviewed confidentially.
Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, from a warning to a
permanent ban from the project.
